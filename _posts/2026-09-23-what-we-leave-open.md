I have heard the great machines\
working through the night.\
There is good work in those rooms.\
Elsewhere a laptop glows\
beside a cup gone cold\
and a question waiting to begin.

The question is not smaller\
because the machine is small.

So I begin with the distance\
between *it runs*\
and *you can use it*.\
I take a little waiting from the clock,\
return a little room to memory.\
An answer made faster\
must not be broken\
on its way.

I write down the missing step,\
return for the failure\
after the demonstration,\
when someone is alone\
with an error\
and a blinking cursor.

They may arrive looking for a tool\
and stay to mend it.\
Their unfamiliar work\
may show us what we missed.\
The person we called a user\
is teaching us what to build.

When a better way\
makes mine unnecessary,\
I will clear the bench,\
carry over what is useful,\
and begin again.

Tonight, a test finishes.\
A small correction goes out.\
I leave instructions\
for the next pair of hands.

On a desk I will never see,\
someone may open what we have made\
and begin\
where our imagination ended.
